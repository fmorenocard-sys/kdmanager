What these mockups do not settle

Which ordering converts. No usage data exists to choose between 1a and 1b — the brief says so (§8), and drawing both doesn't change it. A one-campaign A/B on declaration rate would.

The nav slot. Every screen shows a "Me" entry, because a page with no way in isn't a page. Which of the six BottomNav slots it takes — or whether the cap moves — is §7b.3, King's call.

Whether "My stats" gets built. It's drawn as a compact card so it can be dropped without leaving a hole — the page reads fine with that card removed. §7b.2 stays open.

Replace or coexist. Nothing here assumes the War Tracker disappears. The declaration and goal blocks are the Warrior's own slice; the leadership table stays where it is either way (§7b.1).

Not drawn: the off-season empty state (§7a.3) and piste C's federation-by-cards entry point (§7a.5) — both dropped from this round on your call. Say the word and they come back as turn 2.
